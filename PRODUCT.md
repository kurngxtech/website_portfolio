## Product Principles

1. **Credibility Over Flash:** Lead with concrete artifacts, measurable metrics, and verifiable repos rather than decorative distraction.
2. **Honest Distinctions:** Clearly delineate proven hands-on experience (web & full-stack production delivery) from growing trajectory (cloud infrastructure & application security).
3. **High-Performance Craft:** Achieve instantaneous page delivery, minimal bundle overhead, zero forced reflows, and flawless responsive behavior across all devices.
4. **Editorial Respect:** Treat the recruiter's time with extreme clarity, structured information hierarchy, and scannable technical summaries.
5. **Universal Accessibility:** Provide seamless dark/light modes, bilingual support, full screen reader compatibility, and absolute respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
